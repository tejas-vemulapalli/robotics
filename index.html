Core Concepts

A physical robot is a real world machine that can move, perform tasks, and interact with its area of operation.

AI is a type of software that replicates the learning, problem solving, and decision-making of a human.

The word robot was first used by a writer called Karel Čapek in the year 1920 in his play, R.U.R.(Rossum’s Universal Robots).

AI and robots can work together by using AI for the brains of the team and the robots for the physical output.

Are Humanoids Necessary?

Not all robots need to look like humans because some robots are more efficient when optimally designed. This also saves resources to make more robots. In certain cases human robots ruin the point of making a robot especially in nano-tech robots used for medical purposes like point specific drug delivery. Some common non-humanoid robots include the dishwasher, the printer, and the claw-grabber.

Specialized Non-Humanoid Profiles:
Historical Automation

Leonardo da Vinci’s Mechanical knight was an armoured humanoid machine powered by internal pulleys, cables, and gears. It was used in plays around 14495 driven by an internal or external mechanical crank, hand weights, or springs. It moved it’s arms, legs and turned it’s head but it’s most confusing part for the audience is when it opens it’s helmet. It was the pinnacle of mechanics at the time.

Ancient Mechanics

Early automation was the self-operating use of mechanical water powered machines. In Egypt and Greece, they used water to track time automatically. Hero of Alexandria created a fountain using water pressure.

Evolution of Morphology

Early inventors often designed robots to look like humans because:

  • It felt more familiar and less frightening to users
  • It felt more natural to use
  • It fits really well in spaces designed for humans
  • It proved theories of muscle movement
  • It was intended to spark life in a non-living object
  • It was used for magic tricks
  • It showed off the high skill of the maker with all the complex* parts
  • It was used for story telling using life like figures
* For the time

Many modern industrial machines do not look like humans as it is inefficient to add extra parts that virtually do nothing. It wastes material and energy to add these redundant parts. Modern software and A.I. have change what robots can do by making the robot do it’s own actions with our giving it instructions. It has made robots that move and act (on it’s own will) just like humans.

Innovative Robot Project
Name: Medisort
Problem: Many people with health conditions do not have medical training, so they can easily miss a pill or take a double dose. This simple mistake can lead to dangerous and severe health consequences.
Target User: People who have medical issues and unable to know when to take a pill and how much to take.
Sensors: It uses a time sensor and sets of an alarm when it is time to take medicine.
Rules: It uses the knowledge that people give it and dispenses the right amount of pills. (e.g. Pills in Tray 5 are B-12 tablets, Administrate every 2-3 weeks)
Safety: It is child and pet proof so they can’t take pills not administrated to them. Comes with an emergency backup system so even if there is a power outage it will still deliver life saving medicine. Uses weight scanner to double check for right amount of pills. Health data is encrypted so no hackers can easily steal private information
